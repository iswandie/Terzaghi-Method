Assumptions & Limitations

Professional review required

This tool supports engineering calculations; it does not replace site characterization, parameter selection, or review by a qualified geotechnical engineer. Results are not automatically suitable for construction.

  • Terzaghi one-dimensional primary consolidation theory is used.
  • Immediate settlement: Not included in this analysis.
  • Secondary settlement: Not included in this analysis.
